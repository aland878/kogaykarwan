# Business Research: UK Pizza & More

**Status:** Operating business
**Category:** Restaurant chain · Erbil, Kurdistan Region, Iraq
**Research date:** October 30, 2026
**Official website:** [ukpizzaandmore.com](https://ukpizzaandmore.com)

UK Pizza & More is an Erbil-based fast-food restaurant brand specializing in pizza, burgers, shawarma, spaghetti and other quick meals. Its business model combines dine-in restaurants, takeaway, delivery and online ordering.

This profile draws on the official website, company history, published menu, online ordering system, branch information, contact details and independent restaurant listings.

## 2. Founder and company history

**Founder:** Sanar Karim. The official website identifies Sanar Karim as the founder and owner of UK Pizza & More.

| Year | Development |
|---|---|
| 2020 | First small restaurant opened on Hakmawa Main Street |
| 2022 | Second branch, UK Pizza & More Kitchen, opened |
| 2024 | Third branch opened on Farmanbaran Main Street |
| 2026 | Official website lists four branches, including Jihan |

The founder says the business started with only two people in a very small space of approximately 6 m × 3 m.

The company subsequently reported:

- More than 130 employees
- More than 3,000 customers served (as described in its company story)
- Three branches at the time that history was written
- Plans to expand into other Iraqi cities and potentially international markets

The company also says it sources Kurdish local organic meat and ingredients.

> **Important:** These are company-reported figures, not independently audited financial or employment statistics.

## 6. Online ordering and delivery

UK Pizza & More has a dedicated digital ordering platform (Monu.app) supporting three ordering methods:

- **In restaurant:** customers select their table and order.
- **Delivery:** customers provide name, telephone number, address and email.
- **Pickup:** customers enter contact details and pickup information.

The platform shows product images, food descriptions, prices, food categories, language selection, a shopping cart, customer feedback and order submission. It supports **English, Kurdish, Arabic and Turkish**.

| Fee / rule shown on platform | Value |
|---|---|
| Delivery fee | 1,500 IQD |
| Minimum order | 6,000 IQD |
| Service fee | 0 IQD |
| Tax | 0% |

These figures are as shown in the digital ordering system, not necessarily the current charges for every branch.

### Delivery partnerships

The company says it has used several delivery platforms:

- Talabat
- Lezzoo
- Tiptop
- Toters
- Zuu (announced on the official blog, June 20, 2025)

The company reaches customers through several channels rather than relying only on its own website.

## 8. Customer reviews and reputation

| Listing | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tripadvisor | 4.8 / 5 | 5 reviews; listed #42 among restaurants in Erbil |
| Restaurant Guru | 4.7 / 5 | Google rating; ~3,911 Google ratings in retrieved data |
| Business listing | 4.8 / 5 | 5 ratings in the retrieved record |

**What customers appreciate**

- Affordable prices
- Friendly staff
- Convenient ordering
- Comfortable restaurant atmosphere
- Good pasta and spaghetti
- Food variety
- Delivery and takeaway convenience

**Criticism and potential weaknesses**

- Inconsistent pizza quality
- Some dishes not meeting expectations
- Differences in perceived portion size and value
- Waiting times during busy periods

Some customers praise the pasta while being less impressed with the pizza, suggesting quality perceptions vary between menu categories. These are individual opinions, not proof of a consistent operational problem.

## 13. Conclusion

UK Pizza & More is an established local fast-food business that began in Erbil in 2020 and grew from a small restaurant into a multi-branch operation. Its key characteristics:

- **Local identity:** Kurdish ownership and an emphasis on local ingredients.
- **Affordable pricing:** many items priced in the 3,500–10,000 IQD range.
- **Product diversity:** pizza, burgers, shawarma, pasta, sides and drinks.
- **Multi-branch operation:** four locations listed on its official website.
- **Digital presence:** website, online ordering, social media and delivery platforms.
- **Growth ambitions:** publicly discussed expansion and franchising.
